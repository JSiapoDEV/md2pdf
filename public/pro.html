How it works

  1. You pay once through Polar, the merchant of record that sells md2pdf Pro. It is not a subscription: nothing renews on its own.
  2. Polar gives you a license key, on its confirmation page and by email.
  3. Paste the key below to use Pro in this browser. For the md2pdf-studio command-line tool, set it as MD2PDF_API_KEY.

The key works for one year from the purchase, in the browsers and computers you use. There is no account: the key is all there is. When the year ends, the links you made stay up until their own end date, and new links go back to the free limits.

Your key

What does not change

Pro does not weaken the encryption. A document is still encrypted on your device, and the key that opens it stays in the link, so the server cannot read what you share. The Pro key only tells the server which limits apply: it is kept in your browser, and the server keeps a hash of it, not the key. Privacy has the details.
