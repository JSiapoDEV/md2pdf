md2pdf.studio is operated by JSiapoDev, an individual developer based in Peru, who is responsible for the processing described here. Contact: jsiapo.dev@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Converting a file happens in your browser. Your Markdown is not uploaded to convert it.
  • A document reaches our server only when you create a share link, and the app encrypts it in your browser first. The key stays in the link's #k= fragment, which browsers do not send to servers, so we cannot read it.
  • No accounts, no cookies, no advertising.

Third parties

  • Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the site, the API and the share-link storage, on servers in many countries, including the United States.

We do not sell or share data with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

Your rights

You can delete any link you created from the share dialog, or with its edit key through the API. Because there are no accounts, we usually cannot tell which documents are yours. If you need anything else, such as access, correction, deletion or objection under Peru's Personal Data Protection Law (Law 29733), the GDPR or similar laws, email us and we will answer within the legal deadlines. In Peru you can also complain to the Autoridad Nacional de Protección de Datos Personales.
