Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

md2pdf.studio is operated by JSiapoDev, an individual developer based in Peru, who is responsible for the processing described here. Contact: jsiapo.dev@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Converting a file happens in your browser. Your Markdown is not uploaded to convert it.
  • A document reaches our server only when you create a share link, and the app encrypts it in your browser first. The key stays in the link's #k= fragment, which browsers do not send to servers, so we cannot read it.
  • You never have to give us personal data. Sharing is optional: if you don't share, no document is sent anywhere.
  • No accounts, no cookies, no advertising.
  • The app loads its libraries and fonts from md2pdf.studio itself, not from third parties.

Data banks

Two data banks: shared links (encrypted documents, link IDs, edit keys, creation and expiry dates) and abuse control (IP addresses, for about 60 seconds). Your IP address is processed because delivering a website requires it.

Processors and international transfers

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA) hosts the site and the share-link storage, and processes this data on our behalf on servers in many countries, including the United States, under its Data Processing Addendum.
  • The libraries and fonts the app needs are served from md2pdf.studio, so using it sends your IP address to no third party besides Cloudflare. An HTML file you export loads its theme fonts from md2pdf.studio each time it is opened.
  • Images, fonts or stylesheets that a document or your custom CSS links to load from their own sites, which receive the reader's IP address.
  • If you use the md2pdf-studio tool, you download it from npm (npm, Inc., a GitHub company, USA). Once a day it asks npm for its latest version number, so npm receives your IP address and the tool's name and version under its own policy. Set MD2PDF_NO_UPDATE_CHECK=1 to turn the check off.

We do not sell or share data with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

Your rights

You can delete any link you created from Export > My links, or with md2pdf-studio delete on the computer that created it. Because there are no accounts, we usually cannot tell which documents are yours. If you need anything else, such as access, correction, deletion or objection under Peru's Personal Data Protection Law (Law 29733), the GDPR or similar laws, email us and we will answer within the legal deadlines. In Peru you can also complain to the Autoridad Nacional de Protección de Datos Personales.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 14.
